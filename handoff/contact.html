Quick answers

Frequently asked

Do I need to be a DMC student to join?
The high school team rosters DMC students, but our youth program welcomes families from across the Des Moines metro. Reach out and we'll point you in the right direction.
My kid has never wrestled. Can they still join?
Absolutely. Every Lion started somewhere. Our youth nights and open mats are designed to onboard brand-new wrestlers in a supportive, no-pressure environment.
What gear do we need to start?
Athletic shoes, a t-shirt, and shorts to start. Wrestling shoes and headgear can be borrowed for the first few weeks while you decide if it's a fit.
What does it cost?
Our youth program is intentionally low-cost to remove barriers — annual dues cover insurance and travel. Scholarship spots are available; just ask.
How do I stay updated on schedule changes?
Subscribe via the form above — we send weekly emails during the season with practice notes, match locations, and weather updates.
