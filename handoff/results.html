2nd place at Mid-Iowa Open

A breakthrough day for the program. Brennan and Anderson both reached the finals at 152 and 160, with Brennan claiming gold. Four total placers — our best showing at this event in five years. The young guys learned a lot watching how the seniors handled long days.

Home Dual · Lions Gym

Lions take down Norwalk, 42 – 28

A great Friday night in front of a packed home crowd. The match swung in our favor at 170 when Krause hit a beautiful headlock for a 3rd-period pin and the momentum never left. Reed's tech-fall sealed it. Shout-out to the youth wrestlers who came out for warmups — that's the next wave.

Away Dual · CIML

Tough night at Southeast Polk

A loss that stings — but a loss we needed. SE Polk is one of the top programs in 3A and we wrestled them tougher than the score showed. Bright spots: Frank's 9-2 decision at 120 against a state placer, and Anderson keeping his unbeaten streak alive at 160. We'll learn from this and come back better in February.
